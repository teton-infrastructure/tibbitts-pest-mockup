Quick tips to keep pests away

  1. Clear clutter around your home. Leaf piles, wood stacks and garbage give pests a place to live.
  2. Keep window wells clean. They’re a spider’s best friend.
  3. Trim grass, trees and shrubs away from the foundation so bugs don’t have an easy path inside.
  4. Seal it up. Caulk openings and replace worn weather stripping.
